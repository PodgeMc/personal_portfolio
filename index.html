About Me

P

Pádraig McCormack

Developer, student, and someone who enjoys learning by building real things.

I enjoy creating projects that combine creativity with technical problem solving. A lot of my work sits between game development, software experimentation, and practical system building. I like understanding how things work under the hood and then using that knowledge to turn ideas into something real.

My strongest interests are game development, self-hosting, Linux-based projects, automation, and interactive design. Whether I am building a game concept, hosting a website from a Raspberry Pi, or experimenting with a media server workflow, I like projects that teach me something useful while also giving me room to improve.

I also enjoy VR development and interactive prototypes. Even when a project starts small, I usually treat it as a chance to learn a wider set of tools, workflows, and design ideas.
